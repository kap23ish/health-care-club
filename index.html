Minerals & Ores

Essential minerals and their sources

Mineral Food Sources ✓

Calcium

Dairy, leafy greens, almonds

Calcium is found in calcite and limestone ores

Iron

Red meat, beans, spinach

Iron is found in hematite and magnetite ores

Magnesium

Nuts, seeds, whole grains

Magnesium is found in magnesite and dolomite ores

Zinc

Oysters, meat, legumes

Zinc is found in sphalerite and smithsonite ores

Potassium

Bananas, potatoes, avocados

Potassium is found in sylvite and carnallite ores

Selenium

Brazil nuts, fish, eggs

Selenium is found in selenite and chalcopyrite ores

Copper

Shellfish, nuts, seeds

Copper is found in chalcopyrite and malachite ores

Iodine

Seaweed, fish, dairy

Iodine is found in caliche and seaweed deposits

Manganese

Whole grains, nuts, leafy vegetables

Manganese is found in pyrolusite and psilomelane ores

Phosphorus

Dairy, meat, fish, nuts

Phosphorus is found in apatite and phosphorite ores
